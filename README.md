# Generative AI Learning Lab

This repository documents my ongoing journey of learning **Generative AI** through continuous, hands-on practice — building real projects instead of just studying theory. It doubles as a portfolio of the tools, patterns, and products I've implemented along the way.

## What this is

- A living record of practical Generative AI work: APIs, databases, vector search, and the infrastructure that ties them together.
- A space for experimentation — trying out new libraries, architectures, and techniques as I learn them.
- A step toward production-oriented projects, not just isolated exercises.

## Goals

- Deepen practical understanding of the GenAI ecosystem (LLM APIs, embeddings, vector search, backend integration).
- Move from small exercises toward complete, deployable products.
- Keep a transparent trail of progress — including experiments that don't make the final cut.

---
*This repo reflects independent, self-directed learning and is updated as new projects and concepts are added.*
